Seoul and London: one driving global tech and pop culture, the other a historic financial hub. The 8-9 hour gap requires some thought, but manageable overlap exists.

I've managed this route for K-content collaborations and fintech partnerships. Here's what works.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Standard Time (Oct–Mar): Seoul is 9 hours ahead
  • DST Period (Mar–Oct): Seoul is 8 hours ahead
  • South Korea has no DST — only London's changes affect the gap

The Two Time Differences

Standard Time: Seoul 9:00 AM Monday = London 12:00 AM (Midnight) Monday GMT

DST Period: Seoul 9:00 AM Monday = London 1:00 AM Monday BST (not ideal, but doable for urgent calls)

Common Mistakes

Assuming KST Changes: South Korea does not observe DST. Only London's switch matters.

Not Specifying Both Dates: When it's 5 PM London, it's already 2 AM Seoul next day. Always clarify.

How many hours ahead is Seoul than London?
