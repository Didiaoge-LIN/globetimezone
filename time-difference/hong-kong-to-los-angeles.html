Hong Kong and Los Angeles: one the financial heartbeat of Asia, the other the entertainment capital of the world. The 15-16 hour gap is challenging, but I've managed this route for years with creative scheduling.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Standard Time (Nov-Mar): HK is 16 hours ahead of LA
  • DST Period (Mar-Nov): HK is 15 hours ahead of LA
  • HK has no DST: Only LA's changes affect the gap

The Time Differences
