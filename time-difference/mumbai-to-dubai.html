Time Difference Between Mumbai and Dubai (2026): Close Neighbors

📅 May 12, 2026⏱️ 5 min read✅ IANA verified
Home › Articles › Mumbai to Dubai

Mumbai and Dubai: separated by just 1.5 hours and the Arabian Sea. This is one of the easiest international routes you can manage. After coordinating business between India's financial capital and the UAE's commercial hub, I've learned that this route has its own subtle complexities.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Time Difference: Mumbai is always 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of Dubai
  • Mumbai (IST): UTC+5:30 — does NOT observe DST
  • Dubai (GST): UTC+4 — does NOT observe DST
  • Best overlap: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Dubai = 10:30 AM–6:30 PM Mumbai

Why This Route is Easier (Mostly)

Unlike most international routes, Mumbai and Dubai have no daylight saving time complications. Both cities keep their clocks on fixed offsets year-round:

  • India Standard Time (IST): UTC+5:30 — never changes
  • Gulf Standard Time (GST): UTC+4 — never changes

This means the 1 hour 30 minute difference is constant throughout 2026.

The Half-Hour Offset: A Unique Challenge

The 30-minute component is what catches most people off guard. A 9:00 AM Dubai meeting doesn't translate to 9:30 AM Mumbai—it becomes 10:30 AM (you have to add both the 1 hour AND the 30 minutes).

I once sent a calendar invite for "9 AM Dubai = 9:30 AM Mumbai" to a Mumbai colleague. They showed up at 9:30 thinking I'd meant Dubai time, while I was still wrapping up breakfast in Dubai. Now I always specify: "9:00 AM Dubai (GST) = 10:30 AM Mumbai (IST)"

Common Mistakes I've Witnessed

Forgetting the 30 Minutes: "9 AM Dubai = 10:30 AM Mumbai" not 9:30 AM. The half-hour adds up.

Assuming Same Weekend: Dubai's Friday-Saturday weekend means calls scheduled on Friday need careful coordination with Mumbai teams.

Not Clarifying AM/PM: When it's 8:00 PM Dubai, it's 9:30 PM Mumbai. Always specify to avoid late-night surprises.

Holiday Considerations

Dubai/UAE Holidays: Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, National Day (December 2)

Mumbai/India Holidays: Diwali, Holi, Independence Day (August 15), Republic Day (January 26)

These don't overlap much, so you'll need to check both calendars when scheduling around festival seasons.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours ahead is Mumbai than Dubai?

Mumbai is 1 hour and 30 minutes ahead of Dubai year-round. Neither India nor the UAE observes daylight saving time, so this difference never changes.

What is the best time for a meeting between Mumbai and Dubai?

Business hours align almost perfectly: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Dubai time = 10:30 AM–6:30 PM Mumbai time. The 30-minute offset is minor and easily managed. This is one of the most convenient international time differences.

Does either city have daylight saving time?

No. Neither India nor the UAE observes daylight saving time. Mumbai stays on India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30) and Dubai stays on Gulf Standard Time (GST, UTC+4) year-round.

Why is Mumbai-Dubai scheduling easier than most routes?

Because neither city observes DST, the time difference is constant year-round. No need to remember different conversion rates for different months—the 1 hour 30 minute gap stays fixed.
