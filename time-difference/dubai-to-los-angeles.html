Time Difference Between Dubai and Los Angeles (2026): Bridging the Gulf to the Pacific

📅 May 12, 2026 ⏱️ 8 min read 🔄 No business-hour overlap ✅ IANA verified
Home › Articles › Dubai to Los Angeles

Dubai and Los Angeles: one sits at the eastern edge of the Middle East, the other at the western edge of North America. The 12-13 hour gap means you're always calling someone outside their workday.

I've worked with teams across this route for several years. The key insight? Flexibility is non-negotiable.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Standard Time (Nov–Mar): Dubai is 12 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • Daylight Saving Time (Mar–Nov): Dubai is 11 hours ahead of Los Angeles
  • UAE has no DST: Only LA's changes affect the gap
  • Friday–Saturday weekend: Dubai's weekend is different from the US

The Time Zone Basics

Los Angeles (Pacific Time)

  • Pacific Standard Time (PST): UTC-8 (November–March)
  • Pacific Daylight Time (PDT): UTC-7 (March–November)

Dubai (Gulf Standard Time)

  • Always GST: UTC+4
  • UAE does not observe daylight saving time

The Two Time Differences

Standard Time (November–March)

Dubai is 12 hours ahead of Los Angeles.

LA 9:00 AM Monday (PST) = Dubai 9:00 PM Monday (GST)

Daylight Saving Time (March–November)

Dubai is 11 hours ahead of Los Angeles.

LA 9:00 AM Monday (PDT) = Dubai 8:00 PM Monday (GST)

2026 DST Key Dates

🗓️ DST Schedule

  • Los Angeles: Clocks forward March 8, back November 1
  • Dubai: No DST — never changes

💡 Critical Windows

March 8 – November 1, 2026: 11-hour difference
November 2, 2026 – March 7, 2027: 12-hour difference

Meeting Windows: The Golden 6-8 PM Slot

Option A: Late LA / Early Dubai (Recommended)

🇺🇸 Los Angeles 6:00–8:00 PM PDT
🇦🇪 Dubai 9:00–11:00 AM GST (next day)

LA finishes work; Dubai starts their day. The sweet spot.

Option B: Late Dubai / Early LA

🇦🇪 Dubai 6:00–8:00 PM GST
🇺🇸 Los Angeles 7:00–9:00 AM PDT

Requires LA to wake up early. Occasional use only.

For recurring meetings: Rotate on a bi-weekly or monthly basis.

What I've Learned

📋 Key Insights

  • Weekend calls can work: Saturday 10 AM LA = Friday 10 PM Dubai. Surprisingly workable!
  • Ramadan considerations: During Ramadan, Dubai's work schedule shifts significantly.
  • Friday–Saturday weekend: Dubai's weekend is Friday-Saturday, not Saturday-Sunday like the US.

Common Mistakes

🚫 Avoid These Pitfalls

Forgetting the Single-Hour DST Shift: When LA switches from PST to PDT in March, your 6 PM LA calls become 5 PM Dubai calls. The Dubai team suddenly gets a meeting at midnight instead of 1 AM. Update recurring events.
Not Accounting for Friday-Saturday Weekend: If you schedule "Monday morning LA time," it's actually Tuesday morning in Dubai. The weekend shift means your Monday is their Tuesday.
Assuming All Gulf Countries Use GST: Kuwait, Qatar, and Bahrain use UTC+3. Always verify the specific country's time zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours ahead is Dubai than Los Angeles?

Dubai is typically 11 hours ahead of Los Angeles during daylight saving time (March–November) and 12 hours ahead during standard time (November–March). The UAE does not observe DST.

What is the best time for a meeting between Dubai and LA?

The optimal window is 6:00–8:00 PM Los Angeles time (9:00–11:00 AM Dubai time next day). For recurring meetings, alternate weekly or bi-weekly.

Does Dubai have daylight saving time?

No. The UAE does not observe daylight saving time. Dubai stays on Gulf Standard Time (GST, UTC+4) year-round.
