Time Difference Between Miami and London (2026): Sunshine Meets Fog

Miami and London: one known for beaches and nightlife, the other for history and culture. The 5-7 hour gap is manageable, but DST can still surprise you.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Standard Time (Nov-Mar): London is 5 hours ahead of Miami
  • DST Period (Mar-Nov): Usually 5 hours (both on DST)
  • The Gap: March 8–28 = 6 hours (only time it changes)

The March Gap Trap

From March 8–28, Miami is on EDT (UTC-4) but London is still on GMT (UTC+0). That's a 6-hour difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours ahead is London than Miami?

London is typically 5 hours ahead year-round. However, during March 8–28, the difference is 6 hours because Miami switches to EDT before London switches to BST.
