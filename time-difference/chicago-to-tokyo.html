Time Difference Between Chicago and Tokyo (2026): America's Heartland Meet Japan

📅 May 12, 2026 ⏱️ 7 min read 🔄 14-15 hour gap
Home › Articles › Chicago to Tokyo

Chicago and Tokyo: the heart of America's Midwest and one of Asia's largest economies. The 14-15 hour gap is challenging, but manageable with the right approach.

📌 Quick Facts

  • Standard Time (Nov-Mar): Tokyo is 14 hours ahead of Chicago
  • DST Period (Mar-Nov): Tokyo is 13 hours ahead of Chicago
  • Japan has no DST: Only Chicago's changes affect the gap

The Time Differences

Standard Time: Tokyo is 14 hours ahead of Chicago. Chicago 9:00 AM (CST) = Tokyo 11:00 PM (JST)

DST Period: Tokyo is 13 hours ahead of Chicago. Chicago 9:00 AM (CDT) = Tokyo 10:00 PM (JST)

Common Mistakes

"Assuming the difference is constant": When Chicago switches back to CST in November, your 8 PM Chicago meetings become 9 PM Tokyo meetings.

Not specifying the date twice: Always include both dates in calendar invites.
