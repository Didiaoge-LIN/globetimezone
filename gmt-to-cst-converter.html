📚 Understanding GMT and CST

🇬🇧 Greenwich Mean Time (GMT)

GMT is the time zone used in London, UK. Standard offset: UTC+0. During summer (BST): UTC+1. GMT is one of the world's oldest time standards.

Countries using GMT: UK, Ireland, Portugal, Iceland, and parts of West Africa.

🇨🇳 China Standard Time (CST)

CST is the official time zone of China, used in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and all of mainland China. Offset: UTC+8. China does NOT observe Daylight Saving Time.

Other countries using UTC+8: Singapore, Malaysia, Western Australia, Philippines.

💡 Best Meeting Times for UK-China Calls

Recommended overlap window: 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM London time / 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM Beijing time

The best time for a UK-China conference call is typically late afternoon in China (4-6 PM) which equals morning in London (8-10 AM). Avoid calling during UK night hours or Chinese midnight.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between GMT and CST?

CST (China Standard Time) is 8 hours ahead of GMT (Greenwich Mean Time). When it is 9:00 AM in London (GMT), it is 5:00 PM in Beijing (CST). During British Summer Time (BST), the difference is 7 hours.

Does China observe Daylight Saving Time?

No, China does not observe Daylight Saving Time. China Standard Time (UTC+8) remains constant year-round. This means the time difference between China and the UK varies from 7 hours (during UK BST) to 8 hours (during UK winter).

What is the best time for a UK-China business call?

The best time for UK-China calls is 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM UK time / 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM China time. This gives both parties a few hours of productive work after the call. Avoid scheduling before 7 AM or after 9 PM local time.

Is Beijing time the same as Hong Kong time?

Yes, Beijing Time (CST) and Hong Kong Time (HKT) are both UTC+8. They are identical and don't observe Daylight Saving Time. The only difference is that Hong Kong uses the name "Hong Kong Time" (HKT).
