📍 About Los Angeles Time Zone

🗺️ Location

  • State: California, USA
  • Nickname: City of Angels
  • Coordinates: 34.0522° N, 118.2437° W
  • Population: ~3.9 million (city)

🕐 Time Zone Details

  • Standard: PST (UTC-8)
  • Daylight: PDT (UTC-7)
  • DST Start: 2nd Sunday in March
  • DST End: 1st Sunday in November

💡 LA vs Beijing: Los Angeles is 16 hours behind Beijing (CST) in winter, and 15 hours behind during US summer. This means when it's Monday morning in LA, it's already Tuesday in China.

❓ FAQ

What time zone is Los Angeles in?

Los Angeles is in the Pacific Time Zone: PST (UTC-8) in winter and PDT (UTC-7) during daylight saving time (mid-March to early November).

How many hours behind New York is LA?

Los Angeles is 3 hours behind New York (Eastern Time) year-round, since both cities observe DST simultaneously.
