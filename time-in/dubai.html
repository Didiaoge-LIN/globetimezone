❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What time zone does Dubai use?

Dubai operates on Gulf Standard Time (GST), which is UTC+4. Unlike many countries, Dubai does not change its clocks for daylight saving time.

How far ahead is Dubai from London?

Dubai is 4 hours ahead of London (GMT) in winter. During British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1), the difference is 3 hours.

What is the best time to schedule a call between Dubai and New York?

Dubai is 9 hours ahead of New York (EST). A good overlap window is around 5-9 PM Dubai time = 8 AM - 12 PM New York. Use our Meeting Scheduler to find the perfect time.
