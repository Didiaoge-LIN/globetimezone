Our Story

Built by someone who's lived the timezone nightmare — and wanted to fix it

💭 The Problem We Lived

In 2016, I took a remote role managing a product team spread across San Francisco, Berlin, and Singapore. The first month was chaos. I scheduled meetings at "9 AM" without specifying timezones. I missed critical standups because I forgot about the 8-hour jump. I sent messages at midnight my colleagues' time.

After the third timezone mishap that cost us a deal, I started keeping a spreadsheet. Then a notepad. Then a mental checklist. Eventually, I built a simple tool to do the math for me.

That tool became GlobeTimeZone.

🌍

Why I Built This

10+ years managing cross-continental teams

I've been on both ends of the timezone gap. I've taken calls at 4 AM to accommodate Beijing colleagues. I've sent messages at midnight without realizing it was morning for my London team. I know what it's like to feel perpetually out of sync with your own colleagues.

That's why GlobeTimeZone is free, fast, and requires no signup. Nobody should need an account just to figure out what time it is somewhere else.

✅ How We Stay Accurate

Time zones aren't static. Countries change their timezone rules. Daylight saving dates shift. Political decisions can move a country's offset overnight. This is why accuracy requires ongoing effort.

Our Accuracy Commitments

✓ IANA Time Zone Database
✓ DST updates before change dates
✓ Fact-checked content
✓ Quarterly verification

We update our timezone data whenever IANA releases new tzdata versions. For articles and guides, we review and update content before major DST transitions (March and October).

🚫 What We're NOT

  • Not a tracking tool. We don't store your searches, your location, or your timezone preferences in a database.
  • Not a calendar app. We're a conversion tool, not a scheduling solution. For scheduling, try our Meeting Scheduler.
  • Not a news source. Our articles cover timezone knowledge, not current events.
  • Not affiliated with any government. We use IANA data but are an independent project.
