🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

📋 Summary

GlobeTimeZone does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All time zone calculations are performed locally in your browser. Your privacy is 100% protected.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

GlobeTimeZone is designed with privacy-first principles. We do NOT:

  • Collect personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • Track your browsing history
  • Use analytics or tracking cookies
  • Send data to external servers
  • Share information with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising

2. Local Storage Only

The GlobeTimeZone extension uses your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local or browser.storage.local) to save:

  • Your selected time zones
  • Display preferences (12h/24h format, etc.)
  • Recent conversion history (stored locally only)

This data never leaves your device. It is only accessible by the GlobeTimeZone extension and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the extension.

3. Permissions Explained

storage

Used exclusively to save your preferences locally. No data is transmitted externally.

alarms (optional)

Used to update the world clock display at regular intervals. No data collection.

notifications (optional)

Only used if you enable meeting reminders. You can deny this permission and still use all core features.

4. Third-Party Services

GlobeTimeZone does NOT integrate with any third-party services that collect data. The extension:

  • Uses the IANA Time Zone Database (built into your browser)
  • Performs all calculations locally
  • Does not load external scripts or resources

5. Open Source

GlobeTimeZone is open-source software. You can review the complete source code at:

🔗 https://github.com/globetimezone/globetimezone-extension

Transparency is at the core of our privacy commitment.

6. Children's Privacy

GlobeTimeZone does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any data at all, this is inherently ensured.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Since we do not collect email addresses, we cannot notify users directly. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
