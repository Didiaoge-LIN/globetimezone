AST to PST Complete Time Difference Guide (2026 Updated)

Working with Caribbean, Puerto Rico, and Atlantic Canadian clients means dealing with AST-PST time zone differences all the time. AST is one of the most overlooked North American time zones, and its 4-hour gap with Pacific Time is often misjudged for cross-region US-Caribbean scheduling.

This guide covers the exact hour difference, full 24-hour conversion table, 2026 daylight saving rules, and the most practical Caribbean-West Coast US meeting windows.

Exact Time Difference Between AST and PST

  • Standard Winter Time: Atlantic Standard Time (AST, UTC-4) is 4 hours ahead of Pacific Standard Time (PST, UTC-8). When it's 9:00 AM in Los Angeles (PST), it's 1:00 PM in Puerto Rico (AST).
  • Daylight Saving Summer Time: Most AST regions do NOT use DST, so AST stays UTC-4, while PDT is UTC-7. AST is still 4 hours ahead of PDT. When it's 9:00 AM PDT, it's 1:00 PM AST.

Last updated: May 14, 2026. Cross-checked with official IANA time zone data.

Quick Real-World Facts

  • If it's 9 AM in Los Angeles (PST/PDT), it's always 1 PM in Puerto Rico (AST), all year round.
  • A 3 PM PST West Coast work call is 7 PM AST Caribbean time — late evening for Caribbean teams.
  • Core business overlap: 9 AM-4 PM PST / 1 PM-8 PM AST is the shared working window for US-Caribbean teams.

2026 Daylight Saving Time: Key Dates

  • United States mainland DST schedule: March 8 forward, November 1 back
  • Real-world critical note: Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands, and most Caribbean territories do NOT observe daylight saving time at all. Only US West Coast time shifts twice yearly, but the AST-PST 4-hour offset remains consistent year-round.

Best Time to Call or Meet

Common Real-World Mistakes

  • Assuming Caribbean AST regions use daylight saving time like mainland US states — this is the top AST-PST scheduling mistake.
  • Confusing AST (Atlantic Standard Time) with EST (Eastern Standard Time), leading to one-hour scheduling errors.
  • Forgetting that some Atlantic Canadian provinces do use DST, creating edge-case offset changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is AST always 4 hours ahead of PST?

Yes. Most Atlantic/Caribbean regions do not use daylight saving time, so the 4-hour AST-PST offset never changes year-round.

Q: What is the best meeting window between AST and PST?

9 AM-4 PM Pacific Time / 1 PM-8 PM Atlantic Standard Time is the ideal shared working window for West Coast-Caribbean teams.

Q: Does Puerto Rico switch daylight saving time like mainland US states?

No. Puerto Rico and most Caribbean US territories never use daylight saving time, so AST stays fixed at UTC-4 year-round.
