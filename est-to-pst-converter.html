📚 Understanding EST and PST

🇺🇸 Eastern Standard Time (EST)

EST covers the Eastern United States including New York, Boston, Miami, Atlanta, and Washington D.C. Standard offset: UTC-5. During DST: UTC-4 (EDT).

Major cities: New York, Boston, Philadelphia, Miami, Atlanta, Washington D.C.

🇺🇸 Pacific Standard Time (PST)

PST covers the West Coast of the United States including Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, and San Diego. Standard offset: UTC-8. During DST: UTC-7 (PDT).

Major cities: Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, San Diego, Las Vegas.

💡 Best Meeting Times for East Coast-West Coast Calls

Recommended overlap window: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST / 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM PST

The best time for an East Coast-West Coast call is typically late morning in New York (10 AM - 12 PM) which equals early morning in Los Angeles (7 AM - 9 AM). This allows both parties to start their workday and have time remaining after the call.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between EST and PST?

EST is 3 hours ahead of PST. When it is 3:00 PM in New York (EST), it is 12:00 PM in Los Angeles (PST). This 3-hour difference remains constant throughout the year, even during Daylight Saving Time.

When is the best time to call from New York to Los Angeles?

The best time is typically 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST / 7:00 AM - 8:00 AM PST. This gives the LA recipient a couple of hours to start their day before the call. Avoid calling before 9 AM PST or after 6 PM PST.

Do both EST and PST observe Daylight Saving Time?

Yes, both time zones observe Daylight Saving Time. During DST, EST becomes EDT (UTC-4) and PST becomes PDT (UTC-7). The 3-hour difference remains the same. DST typically starts on the second Sunday in March and ends on the first Sunday in November.

What cities are in EST vs PST?

EST cities: New York, Boston, Miami, Atlanta, Washington D.C., Philadelphia, Orlando, Tampa.

PST cities: Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, San Diego, Las Vegas, Portland, Sacramento, Phoenix.
