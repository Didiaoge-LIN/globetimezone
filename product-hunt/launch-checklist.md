## Product Description (Draft)

**Tagline:**
The Free, Ad-Free World Time Zone Converter for Remote Teams

**Description:**
GlobeTimeZone is a privacy-first, ad-free world time zone converter that lets you compare up to 6 cities simultaneously. Built for remote teams, travelers, and anyone who works across time zones.

**Why we built it:**
Most time zone converters are cluttered with ads, trackers, and unnecessary complexity. We wanted something clean, fast, and free that just works. No signup required. Works offline. Respects your privacy.

**Key Features:**
- 🕐 Multi-city comparison (up to 6 zones simultaneously)
- 🎚️ Time slider to visualize hourly differences
- 🌐 Live world clocks for major cities
- 🔄 Automatic daylight saving time (DST) handling
- 💾 Save frequently used city pairs
- 📅 One-click meeting scheduler with .ics export
- 📱 PWA support (install as app)
- 🇺🇳 Multi-language (EN, ZH, ES, FR, DE, JA, KO, PT, AR)
- 🔒 No ads, no trackers, no signup required
- ⚡ Works offline after first visit

## First Comment Draft

> Hey PH! 👋 I built GlobeTimeZone after getting frustrated with the time zone tools I was using for my distributed team.
>
> **The problem:** We had team members across US, Europe, and Asia. Every time we needed to schedule a meeting, someone had to manually calculate time differences, check DST rules, and inevitably someone would show up at the wrong hour.
>
> **What GlobeTimeZone does differently:**
> 1. **No ads.** Seriously. I hate ad-laden tools as much as you do.
> 2. **Multi-city comparison.** Drag the time slider to see all 6 cities update simultaneously — super helpful for finding overlapping windows.
> 3. **Works offline.** It's a PWA, so it works without internet after the first visit.
> 4. **Privacy-first.** No signup required. No tracking beyond anonymous analytics.
>
> Built entirely as a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The entire thing is about 50KB gzipped.
>
> **What's coming next:**
> - Firebase Auth for cross-device sync of saved cities
> - Public API for developers
> - Browser extension for one-click time checks
> - Notion integration
>
> Would love your feedback! Especially from anyone managing remote/distributed teams. What features would make your life easier?
