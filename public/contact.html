Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report a bug or technical issue?

Use the contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the inquiry type. Please include the browser, operating system, and steps to reproduce the issue. We typically respond within 24-48 hours.

Can I request a new feature?

Absolutely! We welcome feature requests from our users. Submit your suggestion through the form and select "Feature Request". Popular requests are prioritized in our development roadmap.

Do you offer API access or data partnerships?

Yes, we offer API access for developers and data partnerships for businesses. Please select "Business Inquiry" in the form and describe your use case. Our team will get back to you within 2 business days.

How do I report incorrect time zone data?

Time zone data comes from the official IANA Time Zone Database. If you notice an error, please report it through our contact form and we will investigate. Most DST and time zone changes are updated automatically through our system.

Is GlobeTimeZone available for commercial use?

Yes! GlobeTimeZone offers commercial licenses, white-label solutions, and API access for businesses. Contact us through the form with details about your use case, and we'll provide a customized quote.

Can I embed GlobeTimeZone on my website?

Yes! We offer a free embeddable widget. Visit our Embed Widget page to customize and get the embed code for your website.
