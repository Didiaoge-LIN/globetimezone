📚 Understanding EST and CST

🇺🇸 Eastern Standard Time (EST)

EST covers the Eastern United States including New York, Boston, Miami, Atlanta, and Washington D.C. Standard offset: UTC-5. During DST: UTC-4 (EDT).

States using EST: New York, Florida, Georgia, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Michigan, and 16 more.

🇺🇸 Central Standard Time (CST)

CST covers the Central United States including Chicago, Houston, Dallas, Minneapolis, and Denver. Standard offset: UTC-6. During DST: UTC-5 (CDT).

States using CST: Texas, Illinois, Minnesota, Missouri, and 14 more.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between EST and CST?

EST is always 1 hour ahead of CST. When it is 9:00 AM in New York (EST), it is 8:00 AM in Chicago (CST). This 1-hour difference remains constant throughout the year, even during Daylight Saving Time.

Do both EST and CST observe Daylight Saving Time?

Yes, both time zones observe Daylight Saving Time. During DST, EST becomes EDT (UTC-4) and CST becomes CDT (UTC-5). The 1-hour difference remains constant. DST typically starts on the second Sunday in March and ends on the first Sunday in November.

What is the best time to schedule a meeting between EST and CST?

The best meeting time is typically 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST / 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM CST. This ensures both parties are in working hours (9 AM - 5 PM). Avoid scheduling before 9 AM or after 5 PM local time.

Is New York on EST or EDT?

New York uses Eastern Time (ET), which includes both EST (UTC-5) and EDT (UTC-4). It follows Daylight Saving Time, so it's on EST in winter (November-March) and EDT in summer (March-November).
