❓ FAQ: IST and EST

What is the time difference between IST and EST?

IST (UTC+5:30) is 10.5 hours ahead of EST (UTC-5) during US standard time. During EDT (UTC-4), the difference is 9.5 hours. India does not observe DST, so the difference changes only when the US shifts.

Why does IST have a 30-minute offset?

India uses a single time zone for the entire country (UTC+5:30). The 30-minute offset was chosen in 1947 to represent a geographical compromise between the eastern and western extremes of the subcontinent.
