## Keyword Research Notes

### Primary Keywords (High Volume):
- "time difference between [city] and [city]"
- "[city] to [city] time converter"
- "what time is it in [city]"

### Long-tail Keywords (Low Competition):
- "best time for call between [city] and [city]"
- "[city] and [city] meeting planner"
- "schedule call [city] to [city]"
- "[city] [timezone] vs [city] [timezone]"
- "2026 DST [city] [timezone]"

### LSI Keywords:
- UTC offset, GMT, EST, EDT, PST, CST, BST
- Daylight saving time, summer time
- International meeting, global teams
- Remote work, distributed teams
- Time zone conversion

## Internal Linking Strategy

Each article should link to:
1. Main converter tool (CTA)
2. 2-3 related city/time articles
3. Knowledge base articles (UTC, DST)
4. Meeting scheduler tool

Build topic clusters around:
- Americas (New York, LA, Chicago, Toronto)
- Europe (London, Paris, Berlin)
- Asia (Tokyo, Singapore, Hong Kong, Shanghai)
- Middle East (Dubai)
- Pacific (Sydney, Auckland)
