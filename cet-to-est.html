CET to EST Time Zone Comparison (2026 Updated)

Central European Time (CET/CEST) and US Eastern Time (EST/EDT) are two of the most used time zones for transatlantic business. I run Europe-US client calls weekly, and the 5-6-hour gap trips up even experienced international managers, especially during spring/autumn daylight saving shift weeks.

This guide breaks down the exact hour difference, full 24-hour conversion table, 2026 DST rules, and the most practical Europe-US meeting windows.

Exact Time Difference Between CET and EST

  • Standard Winter Time: CET (UTC+1) is 6 hours ahead of EST (UTC-5). When it's 9:00 AM in New York (EST), it's 3:00 PM in Berlin (CET).
  • Daylight Saving Summer Time: CEST (UTC+2) is 5 hours ahead of EDT (UTC-4). When it's 9:00 AM in New York (EDT), it's 2:00 PM in Berlin (CEST).

Last updated: May 14, 2026. Cross-checked with official IANA time zone data.

Quick Real-World Facts

  • If it's 9 AM New York time, it's 3 PM Berlin winter time, or 2 PM Berlin summer time.
  • A 3 PM EST US team call is 9 PM CET European time — late evening for EU teams, avoid long client meetings here.
  • Core business overlap: 9 AM-12 PM EST / 2 PM-5 PM CEST is the perfect shared working window for Europe-US teams.

2026 Daylight Saving Time: Key Dates

  • United States 2026 DST: March 8 forward, November 1 back
  • European Union 2026 DST: March 29 forward, October 26 back
  • Critical real-world note: There is a 3-week gap every spring and autumn where one side uses daylight saving time and the other does not. During these windows, the offset is 6 hours instead of 5. Always check March-April and October-November dates carefully.

Best Time to Call or Meet

  • Top Business Window: 9 AM-12 PM EST / 2 PM-5 PM CEST. This is the golden shared working window for Europe-US corporate meetings.
  • Casual Personal Calls: 6-8 PM EST works for 11 PM-1 AM CEST personal check-ins (best for weekends only).

Common Real-World Mistakes

  • Forgetting the 3-week DST offset gap between US and EU daylight saving start/end dates.
  • Confusing CET (winter) with CEST (summer) in calendar invites.
  • Assuming all European countries use the same time zone as the UK — always separate GMT/BST from CET/CEST.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is CET always 6 hours ahead of EST?

No. During European summer daylight saving time (CEST), CET is 5 hours ahead of EDT. During winter standard time, it is 6 hours ahead of EST.

Q: What is the best Europe-US meeting window between CET and EST?

9 AM-12 PM New York time / 2 PM-5 PM Berlin time is the ideal shared working window for transatlantic business calls.

Q: Do US and Europe switch daylight saving time on the same dates?

No. The US switches DST 3 weeks earlier in spring and 3 weeks later in autumn, creating temporary offset changes during these shoulder seasons.
