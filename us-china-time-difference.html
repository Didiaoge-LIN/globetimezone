US vs China Time Difference: Complete Guide (2026)

Updated May 2026 · 5 min read

China operates on a single time zone — China Standard Time (CST), UTC+8 — for the entire country. The United States, on the other hand, spans multiple time zones depending on location and season.

Current Time Differences

US Time Zone Cities Difference from Beijing Example
Eastern (EST/EDT) New York, Miami -13h (winter) / -12h (summer) Beijing 8pm = New York 7am
Central (CST/CDT) Chicago, Dallas -14h (winter) / -13h (summer) Beijing 8pm = Chicago 6am
Mountain (MST/MDT) Denver, Phoenix -15h (winter) / -14h (summer) Beijing 8pm = Denver 5am
Pacific (PST/PDT) Los Angeles, Seattle -16h (winter) / -15h (summer) Beijing 8pm = LA 4am
💡 Quick tip: China does not observe daylight saving time. So the time difference changes twice a year — when the US shifts clocks in March and November.

Best Times to Call Between China and the US

Finding a good meeting time between China and the US can be challenging due to the large time gap. Here are the best windows for business calls:

  • Beijing 8:00–10:00 PM = New York 7:00–9:00 AM — Best window for East Coast calls
  • Beijing 9:00–11:00 PM = Los Angeles 6:00–8:00 AM — Possible for West Coast (early morning)
  • Beijing 7:00–9:00 AM = New York 6:00–8:00 PM — Alternative window (end of US business day)
