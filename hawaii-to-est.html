Hawaii Time vs Eastern Time Full Guide (2026 Updated)

As someone who works with Hawaii-mainland US businesses regularly, I know how confusing Hawaii-Eastern Time scheduling can be. Hawaii does not observe daylight saving time at all, while the East Coast shifts twice yearly — creating two different time gaps depending on the season. This 5-6-hour difference trips up even long-time US business owners.

This guide breaks down the exact hour gap, full 24-hour conversion table, 2026 DST rules, and the most practical Hawaii-mainland US meeting windows.

Exact Time Difference Between Hawaii Time and Eastern Time

  • Standard Winter Time (EST): Eastern Time (EST, UTC-5) is 5 hours ahead of Hawaii-Aleutian Time (HAT, UTC-10). When it's 9:00 AM in Hawaii, it's 2:00 PM in New York (EST).
  • Daylight Saving Summer Time (EDT): Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4) is 6 hours ahead of Hawaii Time (still UTC-10). When it's 9:00 AM in Hawaii, it's 3:00 PM in New York (EDT).

Last updated: May 14, 2026. Cross-checked with official IANA time zone data.

Quick Real-World Facts

  • If it's 9 AM in Hawaii, it's 2 PM New York winter time, or 3 PM New York summer time.
  • A 3 PM Hawaii work call is 8-9 PM Eastern time — late evening for East Coast teams.
  • Business overlap: No full standard working-hour overlap. The only workable window is late Hawaii morning / early Eastern afternoon.

2026 Daylight Saving Time: Key Dates

  • United States mainland DST schedule: March 8 forward, November 1 back
  • Real-world critical note: Hawaii does not use daylight saving time, ever. Only mainland Eastern Time shifts twice yearly, changing the Hawaii-EST offset between 5 and 6 hours every spring and autumn.

Common Real-World Mistakes

  • Assuming Hawaii uses daylight saving time like the rest of the US — this is the #1 Hawaii-mainland scheduling mistake every year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Eastern Time always 6 hours ahead of Hawaii?

No. During US winter standard time (EST), Eastern Time is 5 hours ahead. During US summer daylight saving time (EDT), it is 6 hours ahead.

Q: What is the best Hawaii-mainland US meeting window?

9 AM-12 PM Hawaii Time / 3 PM-6 PM Eastern Daylight Time is the most balanced compromise for cross-US business calls.

Q: Does Hawaii switch daylight saving time like mainland US states?

No. Hawaii has never adopted daylight saving time, so its time zone stays fixed at UTC-10 year-round.
