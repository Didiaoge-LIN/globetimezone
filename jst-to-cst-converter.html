📚 Understanding JST and CST

🇯🇵 Japan Standard Time (JST)

JST is the official time zone of Japan, used in Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Yokohama, and all of Japan. Offset: UTC+9. Japan does NOT observe Daylight Saving Time.

JST covers: Japan, and is also used as a reference for satellite operations and international business in East Asia.

🇨🇳 China Standard Time (CST)

CST is the official time zone of China, used in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and all of mainland China. Offset: UTC+8. China does NOT observe Daylight Saving Time.

CST covers: China, Singapore, Malaysia, Western Australia, and parts of the Philippines.

💡 Best Meeting Times for Japan-China Business

Recommended overlap window: 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM JST / 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM CST

Japan and China have very similar business hours (9 AM - 6 PM). The best time for a Japan-China call is mid-morning (9-11 AM) when both parties are in the office. Avoid scheduling during lunch (12-1 PM) or late evening.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between Japan and China?

Japan is 1 hour ahead of China. When it is 3:00 PM in Tokyo (JST), it is 2:00 PM in Beijing (CST). This 1-hour difference is consistent throughout the year since neither country observes Daylight Saving Time.

Does Japan observe Daylight Saving Time?

No, Japan does not observe Daylight Saving Time. Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9) remains constant year-round. Japan last used DST in 1951 and has not resumed since.

What is the best time for a Japan-China business call?

The best time is 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM for both sides. At this time, Tokyo is just starting work and Beijing is already in full swing. Both parties can have a productive meeting before lunch and have afternoon time for follow-ups.

Is Tokyo time the same as Beijing time?

No, Tokyo is 1 hour ahead of Beijing. Tokyo uses JST (UTC+9) while Beijing uses CST (UTC+8). They share the same writing system (simplified/traditional Chinese characters) and cultural ties, but not the same time.
