How to Choose the Right Tool Stack

Most remote teams need a combination of tools rather than a single solution:

  1. Daily quick conversions: GlobeTimeZone or World Time Buddy (free, fast)
  2. Team scheduling: Calendly for external, Clockwise for internal meetings
  3. Communication norms: Configure Slack/Teams timezone settings properly
  4. Documentation: Add timezone info to your Notion team directory
  5. Meeting ground rules: Rotate meeting times, record sessions, use async-first culture

Free Tools Stack for Bootstrapped Teams

If you're a startup or small team on a budget, here's your zero-cost stack:

  • Time conversion: GlobeTimeZone (free, no ads, unlimited cities)
  • Calendar: Google Calendar with secondary time zone enabled
  • Chat: Slack free plan with working hours configured
  • Scheduling: Calendly free tier (one event type)
  • Team directory: Notion free plan with timezone database

For most teams, this combination covers 95% of time zone coordination needs without spending a cent. As your team grows, upgrade to Clockwise for automated scheduling optimization and SavvyCal for polished client-facing booking.
